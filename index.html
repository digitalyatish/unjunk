Common questions

How do I restore Premium on a new iPhone? Open Unjunk, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial? On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Unjunk. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. App data is not included in iCloud Backup, so use Settings > Export as CSV to keep your own copy before you change phones.

How do I delete everything? In Unjunk, open Settings and tap Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes it.

Apple Health readings are not showing. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Unjunk, and turn on Blood Pressure and Heart Rate. Unjunk only reads from Apple Health. It never writes to it.

Does Unjunk measure blood pressure? No. Add readings from a validated home blood pressure monitor, or import them from Apple Health. Unjunk is not medical advice.

A scanned product looks wrong. Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a free, open database anyone can improve. You can fix the product at openfoodfacts.org.

Unjunk is a wellness app. It does not measure blood pressure and is not a medical device. Talk to your doctor about your readings.
