Privacy Policy for Unjunk

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Unjunk ("the app") is published by Yatishwar Anand ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. It also serves as our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for US state laws such as the Washington My Health My Data Act.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Everything you log stays on your iPhone. We do not run a server that receives your logs or health data, and we cannot see them.
  • Your app data is excluded from iCloud Backup.
  • Apple Health data is read only, stays on your device, and is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared by us.
  • Two things leave your device, and only when you use them: barcodes you scan (sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product) and purchase records (handled by Apple and RevenueCat).

1. Information stored only on your device

The app stores on your iPhone: - junk food entries, craving outcomes and the optional mood and place tags you add - blood pressure and pulse readings you type in or import from Apple Health - your settings, such as your goal, usual foods, average cost and reminder times

This information is "consumer health data" under some laws because it relates to your eating habits and blood pressure. It is processed only on your device to show your streaks, charts, weekly summary and patterns. It is not sent to us or anyone else. It is excluded from iCloud Backup, so it is not restored if you move to a new iPhone. Use Settings > Export as CSV to keep your own copy.

2. Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads blood pressure (systolic and diastolic) and heart rate samples from Apple Health so it can show them on your chart. The app never writes to Apple Health. Data read from Apple Health stays on your device, is not sent to us or any third party, and is not used for advertising, marketing, data mining or sale. You can turn off access at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

3. Camera and barcode lookups

If you use the barcode scanner, the app asks for camera access. The camera is used only to read the barcode in real time. No photos or video are taken, saved or sent.

The barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a non-profit open food database, to fetch the product's name and nutrition values. No personal information, health data or identifier about you is sent with it. Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your device's IP address as part of the internet request; see its privacy policy at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy. Product data comes from Open Food Facts under the Open Database License and may be incomplete or out of date.

4. Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, Inc. (revenuecat.com) as a service provider to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the app and your purchase history from Apple. It does not receive your logs or health data. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. We do not receive your payment card details.

5. Things you choose to share

  • CSV export and doctor report (PDF) are created on your device. They are shared only where you send them using the iOS share sheet (for example Mail, Files, AirDrop or a messaging app). Once you share them, the recipient's and that app's privacy terms apply.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Their text does not include your health data.

6. What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your data, do not share it for advertising, do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, do not track you across apps or websites, and do not use geofencing.

7. Retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Settings > Delete all data removes everything the app stores on your device. Deleting the app also removes it. Readings in Apple Health are not changed. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as needed for billing, tax and fraud prevention; contact us to request deletion of the anonymous purchase record linked to your device.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or US state laws including Washington, Nevada, California and others), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a regulator. Because your logs and health data never leave your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app: view, edit, export (CSV) and delete all data. For anything else, including the purchase record held by RevenueCat, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Legal bases (EU and UK): we do not process your health data on our systems. Purchase processing through Apple and RevenueCat is necessary to provide the subscription you buy (performance of a contract).

9. Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone's built-in encryption and passcode. We keep the amount of data that leaves your device to the minimum described above. If we ever learn of a breach affecting information we or our service providers hold, we will notify you and regulators as required by law, including the US FTC Health Breach Notification Rule where it applies.

10. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help remove any related purchase record.

11. Not medical advice

The app is a general wellness tool. It does not measure blood pressure and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to your doctor before making health decisions.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. For material changes, we will tell you in the app before they apply.
